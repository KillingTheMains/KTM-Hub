Pull sheet

Read this before you send it to the shop. Pull Plot writes the first draft of a pull sheet, not the last. Every shop cases gear its own way, so the case library is a rental-house 24″-wide truck-pack family — Standard 48×24×26, Tall Standard 48×24×37¾, half and quarter cases, distro racks, dollies — with fixtures-per-case as a well-known house ships them. Swap the numbers for yours. Fixture, cable and hoist weights are published figures; case weights are that house's published empties plus the contents.
What the numbers assume. Circuits pack first-fit at 20 A × 80 % — line-to-line 208 V for movers and bars, 120 V for conventionals and atmospherics — six to a Soca, cut from 5/10/25/50/75/100/150 ft stock plus one True1 jumper per fixture and a six-way break-out per loom. Universes pack in rig order at 512 channels or 24 fixtures per DMX line, one 25 ft node tail per universe and one 10 ft jumper per fixture, a four-port node per four universes on a Cat5e drop. Truss cuts from 10/5/2 ft sections; a dolly carries twelve 12″ sticks or eight 20.5″. Hoists case two to a box with 60 ft of chain. Cable goes in cable cases at about 650 lb apiece. The truck pack is a shelf pack — cases turned 24″ across, four wide in a trailer — stacked to the stack setting and never taller than the door; it's a cube estimate, not a load plan, and it ignores wheel wells, load bars and the fact that your driver has opinions.
Expendables follow a conventional touring list: one safety per fixture plus ten percent, gaff by fixture count, haze fluid by hazer-shows, spare lamps at ten percent of conventionals. Nothing here is a code.
